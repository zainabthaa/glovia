Shine Smart, Glow Right

Oily skin can be challenging—but with targeted care, you can reduce shine and prevent breakouts. Glovia helps you manage sebum production while keeping your skin fresh and balanced.

What is Oily Skin?

Oily skin occurs when the sebaceous glands produce excess sebum, making the skin shiny and greasy. This can lead to clogged pores and acne if not managed properly. Understanding oily skin helps in choosing the right skincare routine to balance oil production and maintain a healthy complexion.

Excess Oil Production

Oily skin results from overactive sebaceous glands producing excess sebum. This can lead to shine and clogged pores. Using gentle cleansers and oil-free moisturizers helps control oil without stripping the skin.

Tip: Look for ingredients like salicylic acid and niacinamide to regulate oil.

Acne and Breakouts

Oily skin is prone to acne because excess oil can clog pores, causing blackheads and pimples. Consistent exfoliation and using non-comedogenic products reduce breakouts.

Tip: Use gentle exfoliants like salicylic acid and avoid heavy creams.

Makeup Longevity and Shine

Oily skin can cause makeup to slide off or look shiny quickly. Using mattifying primers and blotting papers helps maintain a fresh look throughout the day.

Tip: Choose oil-free, mattifying makeup products and blot excess oil regularly.
