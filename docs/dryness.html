What Causes Dry Skin?

Dry skin arises from a lack of moisture in the outer layer. Factors like climate, harsh cleansers, aging, or skin conditions such as eczema can all contribute. Understanding the source is key to soothing and nourishing your skin effectively.

Environmental Dryness

Cold winds, low humidity, and indoor heating strip moisture from your skin. This is the most common type of dryness and often seasonal.

Tip: Use heavier moisturizers with ingredients like shea butter or ceramides in dry weather.

Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis)

Eczema leads to dry, inflamed, itchy patches of skin. It’s a chronic condition often triggered by allergens, stress, or weather changes.

Tip: Use gentle, fragrance-free emollients and consult a dermatologist for flare-ups.

Dehydrated Skin

Unlike dry skin, dehydration is a lack of water, not oil. It can occur in oily skin types too, often due to over-cleansing or not drinking enough fluids.

Tip: Add humectants like hyaluronic acid and glycerin to pull water into your skin.
