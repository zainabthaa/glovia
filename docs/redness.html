What is Skin Redness?

Skin redness can occur due to various factors like irritation, allergies, or conditions such as rosacea. Understanding its causes helps in addressing it properly with suitable treatments.

Acne-Related Redness

Redness surrounding breakouts is a common side effect of acne. Inflammation and bacteria often lead to visible redness. Gentle anti-acne ingredients like niacinamide or salicylic acid can help reduce this type of inflammation.

Tip: Balance actives with barrier-repairing moisturizers to prevent over-drying.

Irritation & Sensitivity

Often triggered by harsh skincare ingredients, weather changes, or over-exfoliation, this type of redness is typically short-term but uncomfortable. It’s best managed by simplifying your routine and focusing on calming products.

Tip: Look for ingredients like Centella Asiatica, Green Tea, or Panthenol.

Rosacea

Rosacea is a chronic skin condition that leads to persistent redness, often with bumps or visible blood vessels. Triggers include heat, stress, or spicy food. Gentle, non-irritating skincare and lifestyle adjustments are essential.

Tip: Avoid alcohol-based products and always wear SPF daily.
