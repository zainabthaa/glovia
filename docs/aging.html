Glow Beyond Time

Aging is natural—but how your skin ages can be empowered. At Glovia, we support graceful, radiant aging with effective skincare essentials.

What Causes Skin Aging?

Skin aging happens due to a mix of genetics, UV damage, collagen loss, and environmental stressors. While it’s a part of life, there are ways to slow, soften, and even reverse some visible signs.

Fine Lines & Wrinkles

These are often the first visible signs of aging, especially around the eyes, mouth, and forehead, due to collagen breakdown and repetitive facial expressions.

Tip: Use peptides, retinoids, and hyaluronic acid to boost firmness and hydration.

Loss of Elasticity

As we age, skin produces less elastin and collagen, causing sagging, especially around the cheeks, jawline, and neck.

Tip: Incorporate collagen-supporting ingredients like vitamin C and growth factors. Facial massages help too.

Age Spots & Discoloration

These pigment spots form from sun exposure over time, especially on areas frequently exposed to the sun.

Tip: Daily SPF is essential. Niacinamide, tranexamic acid, and arbutin help fade spots and prevent new ones.
